About the Show

Show Description

Hot & Juicy Podcast is a weekly conversational podcast hosted by Mapettco, Kabua, and Keithalfred01 — three Kenyan creatives based in Doha, Qatar.

The show covers the topics that matter most to young Africans in the diaspora: food and culture, dating and relationships, identity, entrepreneurship, and modern life abroad — all discussed with authenticity, humor, and zero filter.

Launched in 2025, Hot & Juicy has built an engaged community of listeners predominantly among Kenyan expats in Doha and the wider African diaspora across the UK, UAE, and beyond. New episodes release every week across Spotify, YouTube, and TikTok.

"The podcast where three Kenyans in Doha get into the conversations everyone's thinking but nobody's saying."
