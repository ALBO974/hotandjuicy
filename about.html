Our Mission

The Conversations You Actually Want to Have

We noticed that the conversations happening around us — in our kitchens, in group chats, over late-night meals — were way more interesting than anything we were hearing elsewhere.

So we started Hot & Juicy: a space for the diaspora experience. For people navigating cultures, relationships, food, identity, and modern life with humor, honesty, and zero pretence.

Every episode is a table you're welcome at. Pull up a chair.
