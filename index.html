About the Show

The Podcast That Keeps It Real

Hot & Juicy started as a simple idea: what if three Kenyan creatives in Doha who always had the best conversations just hit record?

No script. No filter. No fake energy. Just honest talk about the things that actually matter — food, love, identity, culture, and everything the diaspora lives but rarely says out loud.
